Starsounds

Use headphones. Each tone plays its carrier in the left ear and the carrier plus its beat in the right ear; the difference is the binaural beat you perceive.

Tones

Drag a tone to tune it: left and right sets the beat, up and down sets the carrier. Each tone's orb also rides on its volume slider: drag it to set the level, click it to mute. Louder tones look nearer and brighter.

Reference lines and the curve

Click a tone to show its octaves (8), fifths (5) and thirds (3). Each line has a glowing point where it meets the curve. Tones you drag snap onto a point (pitch and beat) or onto a line (pitch only). Point-locked tones get a full ring and follow the parent in both directions; line-locked tones get a fainter ring, follow its pitch and keep their own beat.

The Curve slider under the field bends the curve. Consonant: beats rise with pitch and both ears hear the same chord, perfectly in tune. Even: every point shares the parent's beat, a gentle shimmer. Mirror: beats fall as pitch rises, the most tension. A locked tone whose point leaves the field waits at the edge, muted, until it comes back.

Noise

W, P and Br add white, pink and brown noise, which can play together; drag a channel's orb for its level, click it to switch it on or off. Their stars appear in the field: fine fast sparkles for white, a softer shimmer for pink, slow warm glows for brown.

Levels and presets

The Tone and Noise sliders set the overall listening level of each section without changing how anything looks; click their label to mute the whole section. Presets load a ready-made chord; after loading, move anything you like, since presets never change. Your settings are saved in this browser.
