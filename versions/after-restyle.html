Binaural Beat Designer

Click the square to add a tone, up to five. Drag a tone to change it: left and right sets the beat, up and down sets the carrier tone. Double-click a tone to remove it. Hover a numbered circle above a slider to see that tone's thirds, fifths and octaves, or click it to keep them showing. While reference lines are showing, tones you move snap onto them.

Use headphones. Each tone plays its carrier in the left ear and the carrier plus the beat in the right ear. The All slider next to the tones sets their overall level; the All slider in the noise row does the same for noise. W, P and Br add white, pink and brown noise, which can play at the same time. Click a circle to turn that channel on or off. Only one tone's reference lines can be switched on at a time. When they are, tones sitting on those lines get a ring in its color and move with it. Your settings are saved in this browser.
